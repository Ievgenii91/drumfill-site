Rhythmic tracking

Find out if you really played it.

Plug a MIDI e-kit into your iPhone or iPad and play along. Every hit is checked against the grid as you play and marked right on the step you struck — so you see rushing and dragging the moment it happens.

  • On time within ±25 ms, early or late out to 80 ms, a miss beyond that
  • A timeline of every hit, plus a history of each loop
  • Slow it down until the bar is all green, then speed it back up

FAQ

Questions drummers ask.

Do I need an electronic drum kit?

No. Generating, listening, looping and reading fills all work on their own. A MIDI e-kit or pad is only needed for rhythmic tracking — the part that scores your timing.

Which kits work with tracking?

Any drum kit or pad that sends MIDI over USB-C to your iPhone or iPad. Tracking works on one-bar fills.

Can it listen through the microphone?

Not yet. Microphone tracking for acoustic kits is in development and shows as "Soon" in the app.

Does it work on iPad? Offline?

Yes to both. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26.5 or later, and everything works without a connection. The network is only used to refresh the fill library and show the subscription screen.

How much does it cost?

The app is free, including the generator, tracking and all 200 curated fills. An optional Premium subscription unlocks multi-bar fills, sheet music and groove context.
